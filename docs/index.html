#WeaponAttacks / attack ARDPSStr/Dex/Int/Fai/ArcS/D/I/F/A

DPS = damage of one full light-attack chain after the target's defense and absorption, divided by the chain's length when every step is cancelled at the first frame the next attack can start. Hits from the same hand whose windows touch count once; both hands of a dual attack count separately. Bosses: HP, defense and absorption come from NpcParam with area scaling. A bleed proc deals 15% of max HP + 100. A frost proc deals 10% + 30, and the target then takes 20% more damage for 30 s. Resistance rises after every proc, and the boss's own bleed and frost damage rates apply. Buildup decay is not modeled, so frost may be a little high against bosses you can't hit constantly. Powerstance needs two copies of the weapon (paired weapons only need one).

The ranking searches stat spreads in 3-point steps; click a weapon for the exact spread, its leveling path with a DPS chart and its attack chain. Weapon names open the Fextralife wiki. The link in the address bar keeps your inputs, so you can share a build.
